ICICI Bank — Interview Prep Questions (MECE Structure)

For each initiative: 3 layers of depth (Basic → Execution → Strategic) Plus: Critical numbers you MUST know before the interview Designed for: PM / APM / Product-Ops interviews

ROLE & CONTEXT (asked first in every interview)

Layer 1: Basic

  • What is your current role at ICICI Bank?
  • What does the Tech Interface team do?
  • What is the disbursement module? How does it fit in the overall loan lifecycle?
  • What are the 5 products you manage? Can you briefly explain each?
  • What does "post-sanction" mean? What happens before and after your module?
  • Who are your stakeholders? What does each one do in the disbursement journey?
  • What is iLens? What is Track My Application (TMA)?
  • What is FinOne Neo? How is it different from iLens?

Layer 2: Execution

  • Walk me through a typical day in your role.
  • How do you prioritize which product/module to work on in a given month?
  • How do you identify gaps? What data sources do you use?
  • How do you write requirements for the platforms team? What does a requirement doc look like?
  • How do you decide between building one solution vs. A/B testing two?
  • How do you handle disagreements with the platforms team on feasibility or timelines?
  • What metrics do you track daily/weekly/monthly?

Layer 3: Strategic

  • How do you decide priority between 5 different products competing for your bandwidth?
  • What framework do you use for prioritization (RICE, ICE, MoSCoW, or something else)?
  • If you had to pick the single most impactful thing you've done, what would it be and why?
  • How do you measure success for your module overall? What is your North Star metric?
  • What is the biggest challenge of working at the intersection of business and tech in a bank?

Critical Numbers You Must Know

  • Total monthly case volume (~1L+) and breakdown by product (60K PL, 12-15K HL, 30K Auto, 1-2K EL, 4-5K BIL)
  • Average disbursement value per product (INR Cr)
  • Total monthly disbursement value across all products
  • Number of people on your team / platforms team
  • Number of stakeholder groups and approximate user count on iLens
  • How many requirements/features you ship per month on average

1. HOME LOAN DISBURSEMENT JOURNEY OPTIMIZATION (eSign)

Layer 1: Basic

  • What is eSign? Why is it needed in the disbursement journey?
  • What is the Docket Login stage? What purpose did it serve?
  • What is the FinOne Maker stage? Why was it originally there?
  • What is Partial eSign? How is it different from full eSign?
  • Why do NRI customers need a separate eSign vendor?
  • What does "eSign deactivation access" mean? Why would a sales user need to deactivate eSign?

Layer 2: Execution

  • How did you identify that these two stages were redundant? What data pointed to it?
  • What was the user flow BEFORE the two-stage removal? Draw it step by step.
  • What is the user flow AFTER? How many steps were removed?
  • How does Partial eSign technically work? What happens when eSign fails — what does the user see?
  • How did you evaluate and select the additional NRI vendor? What criteria did you use?
  • Who approved the removal of these stages? What was the sign-off process?
  • Were there any risks in removing these stages? How did you mitigate them?
  • How long did this project take from identification to go-live?

Layer 3: Strategic

  • Why was eSign completion rate low in the first place? Root cause analysis?
  • The eSign rate improved by 6% — what was the base rate before? What is the current absolute rate?
  • 6% improvement on ~15K HL cases/month — what does that translate to in terms of cases unblocked?
  • Did you consider any other approaches before deciding on stage removal?
  • What was the impact on TAT (turnaround time) after the two stages were removed?
  • If you had to improve eSign rate by another 5%, what would you do next?
  • How do you track eSign performance? What dashboard/tool do you use?

Critical Numbers You Must Know

  • eSign rate BEFORE improvement (exact %)
  • eSign rate AFTER improvement (exact %)
  • Number of HL cases impacted per month (~12-15K)
  • How many cases were getting stuck at Docket Login / FinOne Maker stage monthly
  • TAT reduction (in hours/days) after stage removal
  • Number of eSign vendors currently live vs. in pipeline
  • NRI case volume per month

2. MORTGAGE ENHANCEMENT PROJECT (20% Manual Touchpoint Reduction)

Layer 1: Basic

  • What is the Mortgage Enhancement Project? What was its overall goal?
  • What are "manual touchpoints" in the disbursement module?
  • What kind of fields were being manually entered? Give 2-3 examples.
  • Who was entering these fields — credit, ops, or sales users?

Layer 2: Execution

  • How did you identify which fields were redundant?
  • How many total fields existed before vs. after?
  • What automation was introduced? Was it API-based, rule-based, or pre-population from another system?
  • Which user group (credit/ops/sales) saw the biggest reduction?
  • How did you validate that removing these fields wouldn't break downstream processes?
  • Did you do a phased rollout or a big bang launch?

Layer 3: Strategic

  • 20% reduction in touchpoints — what was the absolute number of fields/steps removed?
  • What was the TAT before and after this change?
  • How did you measure that the automation was working correctly post-launch?
  • Were there any errors or issues post-launch? How did you handle them?
  • How did you prioritize WHICH fields to remove first?

Critical Numbers You Must Know

  • Total number of manual fields/touchpoints BEFORE
  • Number removed (to get to 20%)
  • TAT before and after (in minutes/hours)
  • Number of users impacted (ops/sales/credit headcount)
  • Error rate before vs. after automation

  • What does the customer see on TMA? What actions do they take?

  • How long did it take to build this from requirement to go-live?

Layer 3: Strategic

  • Why did the bank decide to digitize this journey now? What was the business driver?
  • What percentage of disbursed loans have insurance attached?
  • What is the revenue/premium value this journey processes monthly?
  • How did you decide the INR 25,000 threshold — was there data analysis behind it?
  • What is the adoption rate of this digital journey vs. the old physical process?
  • If you had to increase insurance attach rate, what would you change?
  • How does this compare to how other banks (SBI, HDFC) handle post-disbursement insurance?
  • What was the biggest challenge in building this journey?

Critical Numbers You Must Know

  • Number of insurance cases processed through this journey per month
  • Insurance attach rate (% of disbursed loans that have insurance)
  • Premium volume processed monthly (INR)
  • Self-funded vs. bank-funded split (%)
  • Time taken for end-to-end journey completion (before physical vs. now digital)
  • Number of insurance partners integrated
  • Customer consent rate on TMA

CROSS-CUTTING QUESTIONS (asked about your overall experience)

Product Thinking

  • What is the biggest product decision you've made at ICICI? What was the trade-off?
  • Tell me about a time you had to say NO to a stakeholder request. Why and how?
  • How do you handle competing priorities from credit, ops, and sales teams?
  • Describe a feature you built that failed or didn't meet expectations. What did you learn?
  • How do you decide between a quick fix and a long-term solution?

Technical Depth

  • How do iLens and FinOne Neo communicate? What integration methods exist?
  • What is the tech stack of iLens? (Database, front-end, API layer)
  • How do you handle system downtime or disruptions in the disbursement journey?
  • What is the difference between a real-time API and a scheduler-based integration?
  • Explain how SFTP works and why it is more secure than email for data sharing.

Stakeholder Management

  • How do you manage expectations when the platforms team says a feature will take 2 months but business needs it in 2 weeks?
  • Tell me about a cross-functional conflict you resolved.
  • How do you communicate technical constraints to non-technical business stakeholders?
  • How do you get buy-in from ops users for process changes they might resist?

Metrics & Data

  • What dashboards do you use? What tools (Excel, SQL, Tableau, internal)?
  • How do you set targets for metrics like eSign %, TAT, digital conversion?
  • How do you identify that a metric is declining? What is your alerting mechanism?
  • Walk me through how you would diagnose a sudden drop in eSign completion rate.

Situational

  • If Auto Loan eSign drops from 96% to 70% overnight, what do you do?
  • If the platforms team pushes back on your highest-priority feature, how do you handle it?
  • If a new RBI regulation requires adding 2 more verification steps, how do you minimize customer friction?
  • You have bandwidth for only 1 project this quarter — EL Receipt API or Insurance Journey. Which do you pick and why?
