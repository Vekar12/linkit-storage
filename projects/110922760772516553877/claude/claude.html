Section 05 · Prompting 101

The Art of Asking Claude the Right Way

The quality of Claude's output is 100% determined by the quality of your input. Here's how to get it right.

❌  WEAK PROMPT: VAGUE, NO CONTEXT
"Summarise the complaints we got this month"
Result: A generic paragraph. Claude has no idea what data you have, what format you want, or what action you need. Useless output.
→
✓  STRONG PROMPT: SPECIFIC, STRUCTURED
"I am an Ops Manager at Cityflo, a premium bus service in Mumbai. Here is our customer complaint data for Feb 2026 [paste data]. Categorise by complaint type, identify the top 3 root causes, and suggest one process fix per category. Format as a table: Category | Count | Root Cause | Recommended Fix."
Result: Categorised table with fixes, ready to share in the next ops review. Done in 30 seconds.

Section 07 · Practice Tasks

Learn by Doing: Beginner Practice Plan

Start from Level 1 and work your way up. No coding needed. Just type the question into Claude and see what happens.

L1
Explore the Data
Start here. Get comfortable asking Claude basic questions
"What tables are in our database?"
Discover what data we store
"How many records are in the rides_customerride table?"
Learn to ask about counts
"Show me the latest 10 entries in users_driver"
See actual data rows
"What columns does the routes_routeslot table have?"
Understand the data structure
L2
Filter & Count
Once comfortable, try asking with filters, dates, and conditions
"How many bookings were created today?"
Filter by date
"Show me all drivers where status is Active"
Filter by any field value
"What are the unique values in ride_status on rides_customerride?"
Discover all possible categories
"Find drivers in users_driver where status is 2 (Disabled)"
Spot data quality issues
L3
Compare & Analyse
Now try getting insights. Comparisons, trends, rankings
"Compare this week's bookings vs last week"
Learn comparison queries
"Which route has the highest cancellation rate?"
Ranking and grouping
"Show me daily booking count for the past 30 days"
Trends over time
"What is the average occupancy per route this month?"
Aggregated metrics
L4
Business Insights
Advanced. Let Claude find patterns and anomalies you wouldn't spot manually
"What patterns do you see in our booking data?"
Let Claude find insights for you
"Are there any anomalies in today's fleet data?"
Automated data quality check
"Summarise the current state of our fleet costs"
Get a quick business overview
"Which vehicles have the highest debit this month and why?"
Deep-dive with reasoning
